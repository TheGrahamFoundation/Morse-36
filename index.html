MORSE/36

Compact Machine Intent

Machines should transmit intent, not representation.
The source becomes FTIP. FTIP becomes Morse/36 word(s). The wire carries the words.

THE MODEL
Morse/36 does not compress JSON.
It encodes normalized intent.
1 · RepresentationJSON, XML, YAML, HL7, objects or language. This is where the intent came from.
2 · FTIPThe canonical semantic intent. Meaning separated from serialization.
3 · Morse/36One or more deterministic registered words representing that FTIP intent.
THE WIRE

Words travel. Internal decomposition does not.

Tuple and opcode sketches were useful while exploring the problem, but they are not the intended external abstraction. A receiver sees Morse/36 word(s), resolves them through the agreed registry into FTIP, applies policy, and adapts the intent to its own native operation.

M36 / M366

Bounded, not lossy.

M36 targets a 36-character envelope. M366 extends that to 366 characters when more context is required. Neither envelope permits deleting information that the receiver actually needs. Unknown or non-shared data must still travel or be referenced.
